# Nima Afshar Naderi

**Building practical tools with AI, data, and automation — across restaurants, media, and research.**

I work on systems that turn messy information and repetitive workflows into usable products: operational tools, research pipelines, media automation, and applied-AI experiments.

## Restaurant & FoodTech

### [Diner](https://github.com/nimania/diner)
Persian-first restaurant operations tooling for food cost, inventory, recipes, waste, sales, and operational analytics.

### [Restaurant Intelligence](https://github.com/nimania/restaurant-intelligence)
Evidence-aware tooling for collecting, structuring, and interpreting public restaurant and food-service market information, with explicit methodology and source traceability.

## AI, Media & Automation

### [Jan Kalam](https://github.com/nimania/jan-kalam)
Persian-language news intelligence: ingestion, deduplication, clustering, ranking, source-aware synthesis, uncertainty handling, and a Persian PWA built on a versioned FastAPI backend.

### [Jamshid Panel](https://github.com/nimania/jamshid-panel)
A Streamlit-based internal production control panel for Persian media workflows, connecting source discovery, AI-assisted script generation, image workflows, voice generation, and media-processing utilities.

### [PersianDub](https://github.com/nimania/persiandub)
An Android experiment for live Persian dubbing using device or microphone audio, live translation, and Persian audio playback.

## How I approach projects

- Build usable systems, not just demos.
- Keep sources, assumptions, uncertainty, and limitations visible.
- Use automation to reduce repetitive work while keeping human judgment in the loop.
- Prefer simple, inspectable workflows before adding unnecessary complexity.
- Document current behavior separately from planned features.
